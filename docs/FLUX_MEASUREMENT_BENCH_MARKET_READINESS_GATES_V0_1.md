# Flux Measurement Bench — Market Readiness Gates V0.1

## Gate 1 — Product definition

- [x] Consumer product is defined as a low-energy measurement and education platform.
- [x] Reactionless propulsion is excluded from the consumer claim.
- [x] Existing Flux research is preserved separately.

## Gate 2 — Prototype package

Required before sale:

- [ ] finalized low-voltage schematic;
- [ ] bill of materials with safe ratings;
- [ ] enclosure and mechanical drawings;
- [ ] firmware/data-logging package;
- [ ] calibration procedure;
- [ ] user assembly instructions;
- [ ] hazard analysis;
- [ ] emergency disconnect;
- [ ] raw-data format and analysis tool;
- [ ] known-good reference and sham datasets.

## Gate 3 — Verification

Required before public sale:

- [ ] three repeated calibration sessions;
- [ ] load-cell residuals recorded;
- [ ] sham-load artifact tests;
- [ ] cable-routing and vibration tests;
- [ ] temperature-drift characterization;
- [ ] independent review of electrical and mechanical safety;
- [ ] user test with at least one supervised learner.

## Gate 4 — Market package

Required before listing:

- product photos or CAD renders;
- accurate consumer description;
- assembly time and skill level;
- included and excluded components;
- warranty and returns policy;
- support channel;
- privacy policy for uploaded data;
- clear “not a propulsion engine” notice;
- price based on actual bill of materials and labor.

## Gate 5 — Launch strategy

Initial route:

1. publish the educational specification;
2. recruit a small supervised pilot group;
3. build and test one prototype;
4. record failures and revisions;
5. obtain safety review;
6. offer limited preorders only after the prototype and safety gates pass;
7. expand only after successful delivery and support.

## Current decision

The concept is ready for documentation, funding conversations, and prototype planning. It is not yet ready for consumer sale because no physical prototype, calibration record, safety review, or completed user pilot exists.
