## Description

This public framework separates established special-relativistic time dilation from fictional vehicle architecture and unvalidated propulsion claims. It uses an illustrative Lorentz factor of γ = 365 (approximately 0.9999962c), for which an idealized 4.3-year external cruise corresponds to approximately 4.3 days of traveler proper time during the cruise segment. The framework explicitly excludes acceleration, deceleration, propulsion feasibility, energy supply, shielding, navigation, and instantaneous point-to-point travel from that calculation.

The release is a research-communication and hard-science-fiction design artifact. It does not claim discovery of special relativity, construction of a vehicle, faster-than-light travel, reactionless propulsion, or physical validation of the separate Flux Drive hypothesis.
