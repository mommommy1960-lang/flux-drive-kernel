# Original barrier-transit mechanism packet v0.1

## Purpose

This packet is the first formal candidate for a macroscopic barrier-transit
mechanism in the project. It is a research hypothesis, not a discovery and
not a working wall-phasing design.

## Candidate mechanism

**Field-mediated barrier interaction:** a proposed interaction would have to
couple the sphere and barrier in a way that changes the sphere's effective
transmission state while preserving object continuity and avoiding a hole,
fracture, material transfer, or unobserved path.

This statement is deliberately incomplete. It identifies the proposed class
of interaction; it does not invent a governing equation. The missing equation
is the central scientific problem.

## Required equation package

Before any physical hardware is energized, the proposer must provide:

1. the field or interaction and its source;
2. a Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, or other governing equation;
3. boundary and initial conditions for both sphere and barrier;
4. a conservation-law account for energy, momentum, charge, and mass;
5. a predicted transit probability and timescale;
6. a predicted signal for every independent sensor;
7. a quantitative falsifier.

“Resonance,” “frequency matching,” “quantum,” and “phase” are labels, not an
equation package.

## Testable predictions required

The candidate must predict, before the run:

- whether the sphere remains intact;
- whether the barrier remains intact;
- the expected force and energy residuals;
- changes in temperature, pressure, charge, radiation, and mass;
- the timing and location of the alleged crossing;
- how the result differs from relay, sham, powered-off, and ordinary-opening
  controls.

## Current status

The candidate is **NOT VALIDATED** because no accepted governing equation,
physical measurement, repeated trial, or independent replication has been
provided. The repository's hypothesis registry intentionally refuses to turn
an incomplete proposal into a validated claim.

## What would change the status

The status can advance only through a qualified laboratory review followed by
a sealed, continuously monitored experiment, at least three randomized
repeated trials, a complete uncertainty budget, and independent replication.
Physical RF, acoustic, high-voltage, vacuum, and propulsion hardware remain
outside the software test environment until qualified engineers approve the
fixture and a human operator authorizes energization.
