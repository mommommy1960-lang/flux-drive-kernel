# Flux Measurement Bench — Consumer Product Concept V0.1

## Product name

Flux Measurement Bench

## One-line description

A low-energy, open, auditable force-and-energy measurement platform for learning how propulsion claims are tested and how false thrust signals are identified.

## What it is

The Flux Measurement Bench combines:

- current-limited low-voltage power;
- a rigid reaction frame;
- calibrated load-cell measurement;
- voltage/current monitoring;
- temperature and vibration sensing;
- raw CSV logging;
- analysis software;
- conventional propulsion and sham-control demonstrations.

It is an educational and research instrumentation platform.

## What it is not

It is not:

- a reactionless engine;
- a warp drive;
- a spacecraft;
- a high-voltage plasma device;
- a vacuum propulsion system;
- a certified scientific instrument;
- proof that Flux Drive produces anomalous propulsion.

## Intended users

- physics and engineering students;
- educators and makerspaces;
- science communicators;
- early-stage propulsion researchers;
- reviewers evaluating unusual force claims;
- citizen-science groups working under competent supervision.

## Core consumer experience

A user can:

1. assemble a low-energy test frame;
2. calibrate the load cell with known masses;
3. run empty, powered-off, sham-load, and ordinary-actuator controls;
4. record force, power, temperature, and vibration;
5. replay the data in the analysis software;
6. see whether the signal is ordinary force, artifact, or inconclusive.

## Value proposition

The product teaches the complete measurement process instead of displaying a single exciting number. It makes raw data, controls, uncertainty, and failures visible.

## Product boundary

The initial consumer version must remain low-voltage and non-propulsive in the hazardous sense. Any vacuum, high-voltage, plasma, RF, laser, cryogenic, or flight-related extension requires a separate professional product, qualified review, and additional safety controls.

## Market claim

Safe claim:

> An open, auditable learning platform for measuring ordinary force and investigating propulsion-test artifacts.

Prohibited claim:

> A working reactionless Flux Drive.
