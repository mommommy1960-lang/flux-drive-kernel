# Flux Drive — Hypothetical Residual-Momentum Model V0.1

## Status

This is a speculative mathematical hypothesis for experimental testing. It is not an accepted law of physics and does not establish that Flux Drive works.

## Ordinary momentum balance

For a closed experimental boundary:

[
J_{\text{device}} + J_{\text{reaction}} + J_{\text{environment}} = 0
]

where each (J) is impulse in (mathrm{N\,s}).

A conventional propulsion result is explained when momentum leaves through exhaust, photons, a field interaction, a tether, or another declared channel.

## Hypothetical residual term

To represent an unexplained measurement residual without hiding it, define:

[
J_{\text{residual}}
=
J_{\text{device}}
+
J_{\text{reaction}}
+
J_{\text{environment}}
]

A proposed Flux-like effect would require:

[
|J_{\text{residual}}|
>
k u_{J,\text{combined}}
]

where:

- (u_{J,\text{combined}}) is the combined standard uncertainty;
- (k) is a preregistered coverage threshold;
- every measured channel has passed calibration and control checks.

This equation does not create propulsion. It only defines the quantity that a real experiment would need to measure.

## Required predictions

Before any data are opened, the model must specify:

1. the sign of the force;
2. the predicted magnitude;
3. how force scales with power;
4. how force changes when orientation is reversed;
5. how force changes when geometry is altered;
6. how force responds to thermal and magnetic controls;
7. what result would falsify the model.

## Falsification conditions

The hypothesis fails if:

- the apparent force disappears when cable routing is changed;
- the force follows temperature drift;
- the force changes with magnetic shielding in a way predicted by ordinary coupling;
- forward and reverse runs do not follow the preregistered sign rule;
- the fitted coefficients change substantially between runs;
- the residual disappears when the suspension or reaction channel is improved;
- an independent laboratory cannot reproduce the effect.

## What would count as a discovery

Only a repeatable residual that:

- survives all ordinary artifact controls;
- exceeds the combined uncertainty;
- obeys a preregistered scaling law;
- closes energy accounting;
- remains under boundary changes;
- and is independently replicated

would justify proposing a new physical interaction.

## Current conclusion

The new mathematics can define and test a possible residual. It cannot make a Flux Drive work by itself. The next legitimate task is parameter identification on synthetic and conventional reference data, followed by CAL-00 physical measurements.
