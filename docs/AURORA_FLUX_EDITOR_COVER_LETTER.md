# Draft cover letter — Aurora/Flux formal review

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“Aurora/Flux: an auditable systems architecture and falsification protocol for an unverified advanced-propulsion concept”** as a methods/systems-engineering contribution.

The manuscript does not report demonstrated propulsion. It presents an open software and measurement architecture that separates simulation, prototype instrumentation, and verified propulsion; fails closed when an independent reaction channel is missing; and specifies calibration, uncertainty, sham, orientation, environmental, preregistration, and replication gates for a low-energy bench test. The associated Aurora city-ship concept is explicitly limited to requirements decomposition and systems integration.

We believe the manuscript is relevant to readers concerned with aerospace systems engineering, verification, measurement assurance, reproducible software, and the responsible evaluation of high-uncertainty propulsion proposals. Its central contribution is procedural and executable: it defines what evidence would permit the hypothesis to advance, what ordinary effects must be excluded, and what results would stop the program.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been presented as proof of physical thrust. Source code, tests, schemas, example data, and audit materials are publicly available at <https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/flux-drive-kernel>. A versioned archival DOI will be inserted before submission.
