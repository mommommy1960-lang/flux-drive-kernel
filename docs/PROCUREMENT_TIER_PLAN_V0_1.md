# Low-Energy Procurement Tier Plan v0.1

**Purpose:** define what must be acquired, borrowed, donated, or accessed before a measurement-grade bench can exist without prematurely purchasing hazardous or unnecessary equipment.

## Tier 0 — no-cost preparation

Complete before buying anything:

- finalize measurement axis and mechanical load-path diagram;
- define required force/reaction-force range and target uncertainty;
- define DAQ field schema and sampling requirements;
- write calibration records template;
- freeze control-run protocol;
- inventory equipment available through partner labs, colleges, makerspaces, or borrowed instrumentation;
- identify an independent reviewer/operator candidate;
- request quotes rather than purchase.

## Tier 1 — essential low-energy measurement capability

Required categories:

- rigid nonmagnetic base / stable test fixture;
- calibrated force measurement channel;
- independent reaction-force channel;
- low-voltage current-limited bench supply;
- independent terminal voltage/current measurement;
- synchronized data acquisition;
- temperature sensing at article/mount/cable/ambient locations;
- vibration/tilt monitoring;
- magnetic-field monitoring;
- pressure/acoustic environmental monitoring as appropriate;
- physical latching emergency disconnect;
- reference masses or another traceable low-force calibration method;
- strain-relieved, low-force cable management;
- sham/control article materials.

## Tier 2 — quality improvements

Acquire only if Tier 1 uncertainty analysis demonstrates value:

- higher-resolution displacement monitoring;
- improved vibration isolation;
- better thermal shielding/stabilization;
- higher-accuracy synchronized instrumentation;
- environmental enclosure suitable for benign low-energy control of drafts and temperature gradients;
- redundant sensing channels.

## Tier 3 — external-lab capability

Prefer partner-lab access rather than personal purchase for capabilities requiring specialized facilities, certification, or significant cost.

Examples include vacuum, high-power RF, high voltage/current, cryogenic systems, high magnetic fields, lasers beyond ordinary low-power metrology, or other hazardous apparatus. None is authorized by this plan.

## Quote format

For every proposed purchase or partner-lab service, record:

- purpose / uncertainty contribution addressed;
- minimum required specification;
- calibration included or excluded;
- one-time vs reusable cost;
- lead time;
- loan/borrow/donation alternative;
- whether it changes a scientific gate or merely improves convenience.

## Funding rule

No equipment purchase is justified merely because it sounds advanced. Priority goes to the item that most reduces uncertainty or closes a missing control channel per dollar.
