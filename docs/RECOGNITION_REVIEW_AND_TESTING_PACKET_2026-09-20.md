## Track A — Hard-science-fiction relativistic transport framework

The public framework uses established special-relativistic time dilation to distinguish:

- elapsed time measured by distant observers;
- elapsed time experienced by travelers;
- subluminal high-γ cruise;
- fictional engineering architecture; and
- genuinely speculative extensions.

Illustrative narrative baseline: γ = 365, corresponding to approximately 0.9999962c. A roughly 4.3-year external cruise is experienced as roughly 4.3 days by the traveler during the idealized cruise segment. Acceleration, deceleration, energy, shielding, propulsion, targeting, and mission operations remain separate engineering problems.

**Claim boundary:** This is an integrated hard-SF framework and research-communication artifact. It is not a discovery of time dilation, an FTL solution, a built vehicle, or evidence of instantaneous point-to-point transport.

## Track B — Flux Drive falsification program

Flux Drive is an unvalidated small-force propulsion hypothesis. The public project contains mathematical checks, conventional-physics comparisons, synthetic-data replay, controls, uncertainty planning, and a proposed low-energy force-measurement bench.

The immediate physical question is narrow:

> Does a calibrated residual force remain after ordinary momentum transfer, electromagnetic coupling, thermal effects, vibration, drift, data-processing artifacts, and other conventional explanations are accounted for?

A credible positive result would still require independent replication. A null result, failure, refusal, bounce, or correction is evidence and must be preserved.

Repository:
https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/flux-drive-kernel

## Track C — CHROMASKIN programmable vehicle surface

CHROMASKIN is a separate programmable optical-surface concept. The nearest buildable product is a stationary 300–600 mm coupon or display demonstrator, not a road-ready full-vehicle skin.

The public record is deliberately non-enabling. Private system drawings, construction details, claim charts, and patent-sensitive differentiators are excluded pending an intellectual-property review and filing decision.
