## Reality Audit mission
Reality Audit is a falsification-first research-assurance framework. It is not designed to prove a favored ontology. It asks whether a candidate anomaly survives ordinary physics, detector response, calibration, systematics, numerical error, null models, multiple-testing controls, provenance checks, and independent replication.

**Reality first. Anomaly second.**

A candidate Eureka result is a real external phenomenon that remains unexplained after rigorous attempts to kill it.
