# Macroscopic Barrier-Transit Validation Program v0.1

## Objective

Test the specific claim that a macroscopic T-Sphere can cross a continuously
monitored solid barrier without an opening, damage, hidden transport path, or
loss of object continuity.

This document is an experimental gate, not a claim that the effect has been
achieved.

## Required mechanism statement

Before hardware is energized, the proposing model must state:

1. the field or interaction that couples to the sphere and barrier;
2. the governing equations and boundary conditions;
3. the predicted energy, momentum, mass, timing, and temperature changes;
4. the predicted signal at every independent sensor;
5. a failure prediction that would falsify the model.

“Phase,” “frequency,” “resonance,” or “quantum” by themselves are not a
mechanism statement.

## Measurement enclosure

Use a small, visible, removable test cell with:

- a certified solid barrier panel;
- continuous optical coverage of all six faces;
- independent mass measurement before and after each run;
- synchronized magnetometer, accelerometer, microphone, temperature,
  photodiode, and radio-frequency monitoring;
- tamper-evident seals and a run identifier;
- no unobserved cable, pipe, vent, or access channel through the barrier;
- a second physical barrier around the apparatus for personnel safety.

The enclosure must be inspected empty and photographed before each run.

## Control sequence

Every proposed success must be compared with all of these controls:

1. empty enclosure, no sphere;
2. sphere present but powered off;
3. ordinary passage through a declared opening;
4. dummy mass with matched size and weight;
5. wireless telemetry relay with no physical crossing;
6. sham field settings with the same electrical load;
7. reversed orientation and randomized run order;
8. sealed barrier with the same external fixtures but no passage path.

## Minimum success conditions

A result is not a transit event unless all conditions hold:

- the object is continuously identified before and after the alleged crossing;
- every barrier face is optically covered and the raw recordings are intact;
- no opening, fracture, heating, pressure pulse, or material transfer is found;
- mass, energy, momentum, and timing residuals fit the declared uncertainty;
- the result differs from every relay, dummy, sham, and ordinary-passage control;
- the effect repeats in randomized trials;
- an independent team can reproduce it from frozen hardware, software, and logs.

Failure of any condition means **not demonstrated**, not “nearly proven.”

## Safe progression

The first stages are passive sensing, low-energy mechanical controls, and
calibration. No high-power RF, high-voltage, acoustic, vacuum, cryogenic, or
propulsion hardware is authorized by this document. Any such stage requires a
qualified engineer, exposure review, physical interlocks, and explicit human
approval.

## Interpretation boundary

Through-wall sensing, wireless handoff, optical occlusion, and travel through
an existing opening are useful technologies, but none is evidence that matter
passed through an intact barrier. The final report must preserve that distinction.
