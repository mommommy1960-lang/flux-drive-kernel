# Literature boundary benchmark

## Purpose

This benchmark records what published experiments can legitimately contribute
to the T-Sphere hypothesis. It is a boundary document, not evidence that the
T-Sphere has phased through a wall.

## Published quantum-scale results

Matter-wave interference has been demonstrated with complex molecules and a
native polypeptide in an interferometer. Those experiments measure coherent
wave behavior of individual molecules in controlled beam apparatus. They do
not demonstrate a centimeter-scale object crossing an intact macroscopic wall.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-15280-2

The relevant transferable lesson is methodological: the experiment must state
the prepared state, interaction, boundary conditions, detector geometry, and
predicted interference signal before measurement. The result cannot be
extended from molecular interference to macroscopic wall transit without a
new mechanism and new data.

## Published propulsion measurements

Conventional thrust is evaluated as a change in momentum flux plus a pressure-
area term:

    F = (mdot v)e - (mdot v)0 + Ae(pe - p0)

Source: https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/

NASA technical work also describes calibrated thrust-stand methods for
measuring small propulsion forces:

Source: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20100033281/downloads/20100033281.pdf

The transferable lesson for Flux Drive is that an alleged force must be
separated from cable forces, vibration, thermal drift, magnetic coupling,
acoustic coupling, instrument bias, and reaction momentum.

## Next evidence required

1. Complete the low-energy bench assembly and calibration records.
2. Run command-off, powered-off, sham, reversed, perpendicular, and opening
   controls before any proposed effect condition.
3. Capture synchronized raw force, reaction, voltage, current, temperature,
   vibration, magnetic, acoustic, pressure, orientation, and video channels.
4. Apply the repository's strict HIL and momentum-closure audit.
5. Require three randomized repeats and independent replication.

Until those steps produce data, internet literature can constrain the claim and
improve the test design, but it cannot supply a measurement of this device.
