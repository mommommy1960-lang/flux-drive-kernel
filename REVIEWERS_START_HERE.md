# Reviewers Start Here

Flux Drive Kernel is an executable software and measurement framework for actuator-bench simulation, hardware-in-the-loop auditing, metrology, and conventional propulsion trade studies.

## Review focus

Inspect units, calibration, uncertainty propagation, momentum closure, energy accounting, reaction channels, emergency stops, and the separation between simulation and physical evidence.

## Evidence boundary

The kernel does not establish reactionless propulsion, warp travel, zero-point extraction, or faster-than-light travel. Any physical claim requires independent calibration, uncertainty analysis, controls, and replication.
